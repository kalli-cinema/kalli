# src/renderer

## Purpose

React 19 UI shown full screen at ten feet and driven by gamepad or keyboard. Talks to main only through `window.kalli` (see `src/preload/index.ts`).

## Ownership

- `index.html`, `src/main.tsx`: entry point for both windows: the app, and the player overlay at `#player` (`html.is-player`, transparent)
- `src/player/`: the player overlay over mpv. `player.tsx` mounts one `_components/session.tsx` per video (controls with auto-hide and D-pad focus zones top/seek/bar, loading (fading out over the first frame) and buffering, skip button (chapters, else TheIntroDB via `skips`; ←/→ and the rewind/forward controls step 5 s), next-episode card with countdown, a film's end card (held while a post-credits scene is still ahead), toast, and the black curtain while a quit fades out); `_components/` the seek bar, volume, control button, áudio e legendas and episodes panels, `cinema-prompt` (the 15 s cinema mode question over the first frame; it owns input while it shows), `end-card` (a film's credits: the next of its collection, which A plays with no countdown, three films like it, which A opens in the app, and 👍 / 👎; it keeps the D-pad, A and B while it shows); `_hooks/use-view.ts` (the pushed `PlayerView`), `_hooks/use-episode-start.ts` (ranks an episode's cópias and replaces the playing video), `_hooks/use-after.ts` (the end card's titles, the next film ranked ahead and started)
- `src/app.tsx`: screen composition (welcome, "Quem vai assistir?" once per launch with two or more profiles, wall, detail, search, profile menu) and the play flow (the pre-show first for a film from its beginning when it is on, a rumble on Play, the title opened from a film's end card); reloads the wall's sections after addons change
- `src/who-is-watching/who-is-watching.tsx`: the launch profile picker (avatars, D-pad left/right, A enters)
- `src/_components/`: components shared by screens or by `app.tsx`: `cover`, `poster`, `keys` (on-screen keyboard), `hint`, `btn`, `keycap`, `pad-glyph`, `d-pad-glyph`, `icons` (the app's own glyphs), `lights` (house-lights title card, shows first-run player progress), `notice` (error live region), `toasts` (notifications from main sliding in bottom right, top right in the player), `scrim` (the dimmed area outside a drawer; a click on it closes the drawer: every drawer renders one right before itself), `addon-info` and `addon-logo` (an addon at a glance), `avatar` (a profile's preset picture, else its initial, on its accent), `field-editor` (a text field with the on-screen keyboard for the pad; Perfil and the welcome), `pre-show` (two trailers with sound before a film: TMDB's films like it, else unwatched films off the wall; a reel that won't load or a trailer that won't start steps aside; A starts the film, B cancels)
- `src/_hooks/use-wheel-step.ts`: wheel or trackpad travel as one step per notch
- `src/_hooks/use-streams.ts`: fetches cópias, checks the pick's link (dead ones drop out) and measures its host (and the next pick's; six checks at most) and ranks with the profile's audio and resolution cap (entry page and player); one run per video shared by every asker for 5 min, and `prefetchStreams` starts it for a cover resting about 700 ms on the wall (a series only when the library knows its episode)
- `src/streams.ts`: `playable`/`downloadable`/`trackers` and `playRequest` (a ranked cópia to a `PlayRequest`, with the next episode and the next three playable cópias as fallbacks); `src/seasons.ts`: `groupSeasons`, `flatEpisodes`, `nextEpisode`
- `src/welcome/`: first run (`!settings.onboarded`), three steps (`steps.ts`) driven by `welcome.tsx`, which owns input and the name/link field: `_components/step-profile.tsx` (Criar perfil: name, preset picture and accent on the default profile, the avatar popping on each change), `step-addons.tsx` (the installed defaults, ticked; an unticked one is removed on Continuar; Adicionar por link installs at once), `step-ready.tsx` (Começar a assistir sets `onboarded`); `welcome-rail.tsx` the left column (the mark turning in, step list, player progress), `next-button.tsx` a step's primary action
- `src/avatars.ts`: the preset pictures (`AVATAR_ART`: id, PT-BR label, image) and `avatarSrc`; `src/assets/`: generated by `scripts/brand.py` (`brand/kalli-mark.svg`, `avatars/<id>.svg`), never edited by hand
- `src/profile/`: Perfil, the full-screen game-style menu (sidebar of lucide-iconed sections, options on the right): Perfis, Vídeo (with the projector screen, 16:9 or 2.39:1 CIH), Preferências (with the pre-show, and Kalli's version and update: A installs a ready one), Idioma e legendas (with the room's Som mode), Luzes (pair a Hue bridge, pick its room, or a webhook), Aparência, Controles (in use with battery, paired by Bluetooth with X to forget, search and pair; reloads on a controller notification), Downloads (A pauses or resumes, X deletes after a second X; rows may carry a `progress` meter and a `secondary` X action), Addons (add by URL with a manifest preview, installed; nothing suggested). Perfis has Imagem, a `choice` with `images`. `rows.ts` holds the row model (`choice` with optional `swatches` or `images`, `text`, `action`) and every section's rows; `accents.ts` the accent list
- `src/prefs.ts`: snapshot of machine settings and the active profile (`usePrefs`) and their setters (`reloadSettings` after main changed them itself); sets `data-accent` and `data-cih` (the whole composition scaled into a 2.39:1 band, `styles.css`) on `<html>`, switches the interface sounds, and refreshes history on a profile change
- `src/sounds.ts`: interface sounds synthesized with WebAudio (`cue`: move, select, back, and the curtain when a film starts; `cueFor` maps a pressed `Action`), called from `press` in `input.ts`
- `src/wall/`: endlessly wrapping cover-art wall, Continuar rail (in progress, next episode, downloads; X removes a title), baseline entry and `ambilight` (low-cost poster colour field); a Continuar tile at rest warms its link (`warmPlay`); `_hooks/use-geometry.ts` owns the wall geometry constants
- `src/detail/`: entry page (backdrop with `_components/trailer.tsx` fading in over it, cópias, episodes, play/download); `autoPlay` (Continuar's next episode) plays the best cópia of where the series is as soon as it is ranked
- `src/search/search.tsx`: search screen
- `src/keyboard.ts`: alphabetical on-screen keyboard layouts and movement (`LAYOUTS`, `moveKey`, `typeKey`)
- `src/pad.ts`: pad button names, keyboard labels and arrows used by the glyph components (`PadButton`, `KEY_LABEL`, `PAD_ACTION`)
- `src/data.ts`: data hooks over `window.kalli` (`useSections`, `useCatalog` (each section's first 100 titles kept in `localStorage`, shown at launch until the addon's fresh list lands, and offline), `useMeta`/`getMeta`, `useSearch`, `wallFilms`, history and downloads snapshot, `railItems` and `forget` for Continuar, `usePlayerStatus`)
- `src/input.ts`: input stack and device mode (`press`, `onAnyAction`, `useInputMode`)
- `src/motion.ts`: shared Motion transitions (`travel` spring, `settle`, `leave`) and `tx`/`ty` transform strings
- `src/format.ts`: PT-BR labels and formatting (`copyLabel`, `verdictText`, `VERDICT_TAG`, `facts`, `credits`, `episodeLabel`, `percent`, `ipcError`, `addonFacts`, `languageName`, `trackLabel`, `endsAt`, `minutesLeft`, durations, genres)
- `src/components/ui/`: coss components as generated by the shadcn CLI (`kbd`, `button`, `tabs`, and their deps `segmented-control`, `spinner`); they import through `@/`
- `src/lib/utils.ts`: `cn()` (clsx + tailwind-merge, taught the custom type scale)
- `src/styles.css`: Tailwind entry and all design tokens: raw tokens on `:root`, `@theme static` (type scale, ease, radii, no breakpoints), `@theme inline` (palette plus coss semantic tokens mapped onto it), `@layer base`, and the screens' own CSS in `@layer components`

## Local Contracts

- File layout:
  - One React component per file, the file named after it in kebab-case (`RailTile` in `rail-tile.tsx`); never define a component inside another or a second component in the same file (`_components/icons.tsx`, the icon set, is the one multi-export file)
  - A screen lives in its own folder (`wall/wall.tsx`); components only it uses go in its `_components/`, its hooks in `_hooks/`, its plain helpers in a `.ts` beside it
  - Anything used by two screens moves up to `src/_components/`, `src/_hooks/` or a root `.ts` module; helpers used by more than one file never live in a component file
- Input: the top-most mounted screen or panel owns input (`input.ts` stack); closing it hands input back to the one below
- One meaning per button everywhere: A main action, B back, X download, Y more (full table in `README.md`); no mouse or hover-only affordance may be required
- Button hints use `Hint` from `_components/hint.tsx` and speak the current input device's language (pad glyph or coss `Kbd` keycap); hint strips set `--hint-key` so the key reads one ink step brighter than its words
- Styling: new or reworked parts use Tailwind utilities; screen CSS stays inside `@layer components` (never unlayered) so utilities win over it
- coss: use the styled exports (`Button`, `Tabs`, `Kbd`, ...) and adapt them at the call site with `className`; leave `components/ui/` as generated. To beat a coss inner selector, repeat its exact variant (`[&_svg:not([class*='size-'])]:...`) so tailwind-merge replaces it; app code imports coss files by relative path with the extension
- Pressable parts are `tabIndex={-1}` (coss ones included): the D-pad focus model, not DOM focus, moves between them
- Hover steers focus only after a real pointer move (`pointerMoved`), and not after a wheel step until the pointer travels 24 px; Big Picture hides the cursor only in pad mode, and Esc on the wall (keyboard) leaves it
- Season tabs are coss `Tabs` (underline) driven by LB/RB; the strip slides to keep the current season in view
- Layout scale: root font-size is 1/120 of the viewport width, so sizes are in rem and the composition is identical from 1080p to 4K
- Visual tokens in `styles.css` `:root` must match `DESIGN.md`; edition yellow (`--edition`) is reserved for focus, progress and the one primary action; `--alert` only for errors; `--boost` (red) only for volume past 100%
- A profile's accent replaces `--edition` through `:root[data-accent]` rules in `styles.css` (Tailwind 400 colours); `profile/accents.ts` lists the same keys and colours, so a new accent is added in both plus `Accent` in `shared/types.ts`
- Icons: `lucide-react` (stroke 1.75) for the profile menu's sections, addon placeholders and the player's controls; `_components/icons.tsx` for the app's own glyphs
- There is no "Ajustes": everything configurable lives in Perfil (Menu); copy points there as "Perfil → <seção>"
- Motion uses `motion.ts` presets; honour `prefers-reduced-motion` (handled at the end of `styles.css` and by `MotionConfig`)
- Anything that travels animates a `transform` string (`tx`/`ty`), never Motion's `x`/`y` shorthands, which run on the main thread; exits use `leave`, faster than the entrance; no `ease-in` on UI
- All user-facing copy in PT-BR
- Performance budget: 60 fps on a Vega 8 iGPU at 1080p and an RX 7600 at 4K; avoid full-screen blur filters and per-frame layout work
- An effect never returns an expression's value: Chromium's `scrollIntoView` returns a Promise, and a non-function cleanup crashes React (the whole overlay); use a `void` or a block body
- Interface sounds stay soft and short, never in the player overlay; the trailer plays only while its page is active (never under a film), never with reduced motion, and shows only once YouTube reports it playing. The CSP allows frames from `http://127.0.0.1:*` for it, nothing else
- The overlay's Áudio e legendas panel has a third column, Som, only when audio is decoded here; a download ahead of the next episode is requested from `session.tsx` (Preferências → Baixar o próximo episódio)
